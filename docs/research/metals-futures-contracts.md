# Metals futures: contract terms for a general trade representation

Reviewed 14 September 2026. This survey covers exchange-listed metals contracts and follows the [petroleum](petroleum-futures-contracts.md), [gas/NGL](natural-gas-and-ngl-futures-contracts.md) and [power](power-futures-contracts.md) reviews. It is evidence for the eventual trade template, not an approved constructor specification.

The major additions are **LME prompt dates and margin treatment**, **fine versus gross metal quantity**, and **delivery through warrants or fractional warehouse interests**. Cash-settled metal indexes, regional premiums and currency conventions also recur. A metal name alone does not determine any of these terms.

## The universe and its contract groups

| Commodity group | Representative coverage |
|---|---|
| Precious metals | Gold, silver, platinum and palladium; standard, mini/micro and cash-linked variants. |
| Base/non-ferrous metals | Copper, aluminium, zinc, lead, nickel and tin; primary metal and aluminium alloys. |
| Ferrous metals and inputs | Steel coil, rebar, scrap and iron ore; physical goods and cash price assessments. |
| Battery materials and other industrial inputs | Cobalt, lithium compounds, alumina, molybdenum and silicon where listed. These include compounds and mineral inputs, not just elemental metal. |
| Regional premiums and differentials | The additional price for a specified region, delivery basis or grade; separate from the outright metal price. |

LME and COMEX/NYMEX are central examples. SHFE, INE, GFEX, SGX, Osaka Exchange, MCX, TFEX and B3 extend the coverage across physical-delivery conventions, currencies and price references. Presence in a rulebook establishes published terms, not liquidity or availability of every dated series. Options, OTC bullion accounts, leases, mine offtakes and equity/ETF exposures are outside this futures review.

| Contract structure | Examples / why it matters |
|---|---|
| Physical contract for a specific date | LME prompt-date metals; an actual settlement date is necessary. |
| Physical contract for a delivery month | COMEX bullion/base metals and Asian physical futures; notice/tender/delivery periods are separate events. |
| Fractional warehouse-interest delivery | Some precious-metal micros settle through interests in larger warrants. |
| Cash reference to another future | Small gold/copper contracts; exact referenced maturity and fixing event matter. |
| Cash assessment/index average | Steel, ore, lithium and cobalt; publication window and assessment identity define the price. |
| Premium/differential | A regional premium can be a directly published reference rather than a subtraction computed by the exchange. |
| Currency-converted or fixed-currency multiplier | Quote currency, payment currency and FX treatment are independent terms. |

Units below: `t` = metric tonne; `st` = U.S. short ton (2,000 lb); gross/long ton = 2,240 lb; `dmt` = dry metric tonne; `lb` = pound; `oz t` = troy ounce. A troy ounce is not the ordinary avoirdupois ounce. Purity is a separate measurement dimension, not a unit conversion. Price increments are quoted-price increments unless otherwise stated. [UNECE unit definitions](https://service.unece.org/trade/uncefact/vocabulary/rec20/)

**Prompt date means an actual settlement date.** “Cash” identifies the settlement business day after tomorrow; it does not mean cash settlement instead of physical delivery. “Three month” is a rolling tenor label whose resolved date changes with the observation day. Weekly prompts usually fall on Wednesday and monthly prompts on the third Wednesday, with exchange substitutions. A monthly prompt is a date, rather than necessarily a whole month of delivery or averaging. [LME reference-data guide](https://www.lme.com/-/media/files/trading/systems/lmesource/market-data-guide-for-ref-data-1-1.pdf), [prompt structure](https://www.lme.com/sustainability-and-physical-markets/physical-market-benefits/prompt-date-structure)

Physical delivery transfers eligible **LME warrants**. Warrant weights have a permitted ±2% tolerance; nominal lot size and actual delivered weight are separate. Approved brand, form and warehouse eligibility determine what can satisfy delivery. [LME warrants](https://www.lme.com/sustainability-and-physical-markets/warehousing/lme-warrants)

**The margin exception also applies to cash-settled MAF.** June 2026 clearing rules assign **discounted contingent variation margin (DCVM)** to Exchange Traded Forwards and Monthly Average Futures. Contingent profit credits can offset margin liabilities but cannot be withdrawn as cash. Other Exchange Traded Futures use realized variation margin. Final cash/physical settlement therefore does not determine daily cash-flow treatment. LMEmini's clearing classification needs explicit onboarding verification. [LME Clear rules C4.2–C 4.6](https://www.lme.com/-/media/files/clearing/rules-and-regulations/22-06-2026-lme-clear-rulebook.pdf)

Governing rules place LMEmini payment **on prompt**, despite webpage wording suggesting last-trading-day cash settlement. Standard final payments for European premiums, Turkey scrap and lithium are next Settlement Business Day after prompt; U.S. Midwest is second Settlement Business Day. Publication delays can alter payment. Lithium's July 2026 rulebook cutoff is 16:00, while its page says 16:30; retain this discrepancy. [July 2026 exchange rules, Parts 3/6B/6C/6D](https://www.lme.com/-/media/files/company/market-regulation/rulebook/evergreen-for-non-rulebook-pages/rulebook-as-of-31-july-2026.pdf)

The physical products above generally finish trading on M's third-last business day, while delivery can occur throughout M, including before expiry. Delivery transfers a warrant or eligible receipt interest; withdrawing metal from storage is a separate action. Notice-of-intent pricing, late-month exceptions and storage adjustments enter invoicing. [Joint delivery rules, chapter 7](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/NYMEX/1/7.pdf)

CME's delivery guide explicitly illustrates **100 gross ounces ×0.9999 fineness =99.99 fine ounces** for a gold invoice. A nominal 100-oz futures lot is therefore not a promise that every invoice uses exactly 100 fine ounces. Preserve actual measured delivery quantities and prescribed rounded lot constants. [Precious-metals delivery guide](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/metals/files/precious-metals-physical-delivery-process-2025.pdf)

For the SHFE cases, the ordinary last trading day is the **15th of the delivery month**, subject to exchange holiday adjustments and special announcements. The delivery windows below use the current Chinese rules, effective January 2026; they are not interchangeable across products.

**The ore benchmark has changed.** Platts moved IODEX from **62% to 61% Fe effective 2 January 2026**, alongside other quality changes. “Iron ore 62%” is therefore an unsafe permanent identity for a contract referencing IODEX. [Publisher notice](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/pricing-benchmarks/our-methodology/subscriber-notes/010226-platts-updates-iodex-quality-specifications-to-reflect-61-fe-effective-jan-2-2026)

SGX also has a useful **lump-premium** case: the current tick rule quotes it in USD per **dry metric tonne unit, dmtu**, rather than simply USD/t. The older product table's 6,200-dmtu contract coefficient has not been confirmed against current terms; do not infer it from the revised outright ore benchmark. [Current SGX quotation rule](https://rulebook.sgx.com/rulebook/appendix-b-regulatory-notice-4111), [older product table](https://www.sgx.com/zh-hans/derivatives/products/iron-ore?cc=FE)

These broaden market coverage without necessarily requiring another trade structure. Their full current specifications were not independently transcribed in this survey. SGX's statistics establish reporting presence, not liquidity. For TFEX's ordinary gold contracts, the specified price formula is:

```text
THB per gold-baht weight = LBMA_AM_USD_per_oz
                         × (15.244 / 31.1035)
                         × (0.965 / 0.995)
                         × specified_THB_per_USD
```

These are the contract's conversion constants; do not substitute an assumed universal fineness basis. GO/MGO instead apply their fixed THB multiplier to the numerical USD gold-price movement. The first structure uses an observed FX rate; the second is a fixed-currency, or quanto, payoff. [Ordinary gold terms](https://www.tfex.co.th/en/products/precious-metal/gold-futures/contract-specification), [GO rules](https://media.tfex.co.th/rulebook/regulation/Chapter 600TFEX_Procedures_ListingofDerivativesContracts.pdf)

## Consequences for the eventual trade representation

### Dated contract identity

Represent a specific contract instance separately from a rolling market-data label. LME “three month” is a tenor used to find a prompt date at a particular observation time. A booked trade retains its resolved date. A calendar-month future instead needs its own last-trade, notice/tender, delivery and final-price dates. A label such as “cash” or “mini” must not choose settlement mechanics.

### Quantity, measurement and the payment multiplier

Keep nominal trading quantity, price denominator, minimum delivery unit and actual invoice quantity distinct. For ordinary contracts the cash multiplier follows from dimensional conversion, such as kilograms to quoted grams, or pounds to quoted pounds. For a fixed-currency product such as GO, the monetary multiplier is itself a contract term.

Separate **gross bar weight**, **fine contained metal**, permitted quantity tolerance and assay/fineness. Dry tonnes additionally need the contractual moisture basis; manufactured steel can use **theoretical weight** rather than scale weight. Tonnes of lithium carbonate are not tonnes of lithium metal. A named grade or percentage is not permission to invent a conversion between different products.

For physical delivery, the contract definition needs the permitted grade, forms, approved brands, warehouse network, delivery-unit rules and applicable premiums/discounts. The particular warrant, bar, assay and measured weight belong to the delivered allocation/state. Receipt transfer, fractional warrant entitlement and warehouse load-out are different events.

### Price definition and currency

Distinguish an outright metal price, regional premium, computed spread, single benchmark fixing, futures opening/closing price and published assessment average. Keep source, location, grade, duty/tax basis, observation window, weights and rounding. A premium-only contract does not automatically include the LME base price. An exchange spread order that creates two positions remains two positions.

Quote currency and payment currency may differ. Floating FX conversion, a directly published local-currency index and a fixed-currency multiplier require separate definitions. Contractual purity/mass conversions and FX conversion must retain their specified order and constants.

### Margin and settlement are contract facts

The template must be able to identify the relevant margin/cash-flow regime, with collateral accounting separate from realized payments. In particular, an LME contingent mark-to-market credit must not be booked as daily spendable cash. Final physical invoicing and cash-index settlement also remain separate from initial margin and daily risk calculations.

This does not put a pricing algorithm into the trade record. It records the economic obligations and settlement treatment; forecasting, discounting and computation remain outside it, consistent with [Principle 7](../../PRINCIPLES.md).

| Candidate contract block | Metals-specific content |
|---|---|
| Identity and dates | Venue/product/version, actual prompt date or dated monthly series, independent event calendars. |
| Quantity and price scale | Lot, mass unit, fine/gross/dry basis, quote denominator, cash multiplier, delivery-unit multiple. |
| Price reference | Metal/compound/grade, source and location, premium or outright, fixing/average definition, tax basis. |
| Settlement obligations | Cash/physical/fractional receipt, eligible delivery specifications, invoice adjustments and payment currency. |
| Margin treatment | Realized daily settlement versus contingent margin treatment and prompt-date realization. |
| State | Known fixings, settled cash, collateral, notice/delivery allocations, actual weights and warrant/receipt interests. |

## Coverage and source qualifications

This is a broad survey of representative **contract structures**, not a complete security master. Rows with primary specifications support the terms shown; catalogue-only coverage and partial verification are identified separately. Published terms do not establish trading activity or participant delivery eligibility. Exchange rules and effective-date notices take precedence over summary pages.

- **CME small bullion timing:** Chapters 131/130 put last trade in the month preceding the named month, but literally reference the standard future's third-last-business-day fixing in the named month. CME's operational gold guidance says cash settlement occurs upon expiry. The precise final observation date needs exchange clarification before implementing these two products. [Gold chapter 131](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/COMEX/1a/131.pdf), [silver chapter 130](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/COMEX/1a/130.pdf), [CME operational guidance](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457088147/Gold)
- **LME:** The mini payment wording and lithium intraday cutoff conflicts are recorded above. Confirm LMEmini's clearing classification before assigning a margin model. The governing exchange and clearing rulebooks are separate authorities.
- **China:** Current SHFE Chinese rules replace older English delivery-period descriptions. GFEX detailed quality schedules and DCE's full current specification remain incomplete. A SHFE catalogue entry dated 24 September 2026 is after this review date and has not been treated as operative.
- **SGX and benchmark versions:** Use the January 2026 IODEX specification rather than the older 62%-labelled product table. The current lump-premium multiplier and full terms for the additional catalogue products remain unverified. Prospective JPX and B3 changes are dated explicitly above.

No constructor, pricing code or existing repository principle was changed by this research.
