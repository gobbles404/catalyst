# Carbon and environmental futures: contract terms for a general trade representation

Reviewed 14 September 2026. Companion to the [petroleum](petroleum-futures-contracts.md), [gas/NGL](natural-gas-and-ngl-futures-contracts.md), [power](power-futures-contracts.md), [metals](metals-futures-contracts.md) and [agriculture](agricultural-futures-contracts.md) surveys. Scope is exchange-listed futures, including adjacent environmental products where they introduce useful contract distinctions. This is research for the eventual trade template, not an implemented constructor specification.

The defining feature is **a scheme-defined unit or certificate, with contractual eligibility rules**. Equal quantities of carbon dioxide equivalent do not make two instruments interchangeable. A futures delivery usually transfers registry units; it does not transport gas, supply electricity or automatically retire credits against an emissions claim.

## Groups to cover

| Group | What the underlying represents | Representative futures |
|---|---|---|
| Emissions allowances | A permission to emit under a particular scheme | EU EUA and EUA2, UKA, California CCA, Washington WCA, RGGI and New Zealand NZU. |
| Carbon credits and offsets | A credited reduction or removal, subject to a program's issuance and eligibility conditions | CME GEO/N-GEO, ICE and Abaxx CORSIA baskets, ASX ACCU, California offsets and removal baskets. |
| Emissions-performance credits | Credited performance against a scheme's emissions benchmark | Alberta AEPC futures; distinct from a project-offset basket. |
| Fuel carbon-intensity credits | Compliance units generated under a fuel program | California LCFS and regional clean-fuel credits. |
| Renewable energy certificates | Attributes of qualifying renewable generation | U.S. RECs, European Guarantees of Origin (GOs) and Australian LGCs. |
| Renewable fuel identifiers | Qualifying renewable-fuel compliance units | U.S. Renewable Identification Numbers (RINs), distinguished by fuel category and vintage. |
| Other emissions programs | Pollutant-specific allowances | SO2 and NOx products require separate current-listing verification. They are not carbon units. |

**Compliance versus voluntary is a separate classification from allowance versus credit.** Credits can serve compliance purposes: CORSIA is an aviation compliance framework; ACCUs and California offsets also have compliance applications. Likewise, a renewable energy certificate denominated in MWh is not a carbon offset denominated in tCO2e.

The principal venues reviewed are ICE Futures Europe, ICE Futures U.S., EEX, CME/NYMEX, Nodal, Abaxx and ASX. Cash-index futures and auction-linked conversion futures are settlement structures cutting across these groups. Auctions, spot transfers, bilateral offtake agreements, options and environmental equities are outside the futures inventory. Listing evidence does not establish liquidity.

`M` means the futures contract month; `tCO2e` means a metric tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent. RGGI instead uses a **short ton of CO2**. Ticks below are increments in quoted price. The contract's named vintage, the underlying unit's vintage and the futures expiry are separate facts.

For the ICE U.S. month-end convention, the final weekday of December is excluded as a business day unless the exchange directs otherwise. This must be represented in the appropriate product calendar. ICE U.S. allowance increments were cross-checked against the rulebook's tick table. [ICE Chapter 18, Resolution 1](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/rulebooks/futures_us/18._US_Gas_Power_and_Environmental_Contracts.pdf)

Delivery does not imply simultaneous payment. ICE's monthly EUA/EUA2/UKA process ordinarily collects buyer payment at expiry +1 business day; seller payment depends on receipt at +2 or +3. The U.S. allowance process in Part N1 instead places buyer payment and seller delivery at +2, with seller payment and initiation of buyer delivery at +3. Applicable delivery parts and permitted delays differ. [ICE Clear delivery procedures, A/A1/N/N1](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/clear_europe/rulebooks/procedures/Delivery_Procedures.pdf)

The auction-linked products need a separate conversion rule: identity and maturity of the assigned future, assignment price, publication event and disruption fallback. The sources establish assignment at auction settlement price; this survey has not resolved all cashflows of the traded premium/discount. **Do not assume the assigned price is auction price plus the traded spread.**

Allowance exposure also has cash and spread variants: Nodal lists financial CCA futures and a **Washington-versus-California allowance OPIS spread future**. These are included at catalogue level; their detailed assessment formulas were not transcribed here. [Nodal/IncubEx product listing](https://theincubex.com/north-american-products/)

## Carbon credits and offsets

Carbon-credit baskets require more than a registry name: project category, vintage window, labels and eligibility for a compliance phase can determine whether a unit is deliverable.

California compliance offsets illustrate the invalidation distinction directly. Nodal **CCO0 / CCO8** each has a **1,000-offset** lot, USD/offset quotation and USD 0.01 tick. Both physically deliver through CITSS and permit eligible CCA allowances as substitutes. CCO0 requires offsets beyond the contractual invalidation window; CCO8 does not impose that restriction. Last trade is three business days before the last business day of M, four in December; delivery follows three business days later. [CCO0 specification](https://theincubex.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/California-Carbon-Offset-0-Future.pdf), [CCO8 specification](https://theincubex.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/California-Carbon-Offset-8-Future.pdf)

Nodal also lists Direct Environmental Benefits (DEBS) variants. The CCO0 DEBS basket adds California-benefit eligibility and does not offer the same general CCA substitution. Thus even **allowance versus offset** can be a basket choice only where the particular contract permits it. [DEBS specification](https://theincubex.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/California-Carbon-Offset-0-with-Direct-Environmental-Benefits-DEBS-Future.pdf), [current Nodal catalogue](https://www.nodalexchange.com/products-services/environmental/)

Current rules separately limit **DEBS8** to an invalidation timeframe of at most eight years and exclude CCA substitution. The plain CCO8 name should not be interpreted as exactly eight years of remaining invalidation exposure. [Current Appendix A, pp. 3504–3511](https://www.nodalexchange.com/wp-content/uploads/nodal-exchange-contract-specifications-rulebook-appendix-a.pdf)

ICE CORSIA normally expires on the last Monday, with earlier-Monday adjustments for contractual holidays. Delivery runs from +1 through +3 business days, with seller instructions and buyer payment due earlier. Delivery price uses exchange settlement on last trade, subject to exchange discretion. [ICE procedures 7O2](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/contractregs/214_SECTION_7O2.pdf)

Abaxx's beginning-of-month date has an additional preceding-business-day U.S. bank-holiday condition. Eligible custodians may participate in delivery: the exchange confirmed a July 2026 CP1 delivery involving Climate Impact X. That is an **Abaxx** future, not evidence of an SGX-listed future. [Abaxx chapter 1400](https://abexcws.directus.app/assets/bbc2693a-8fc5-44c3-92c2-8390ea49f708), [delivery confirmation](https://abaxx.exchange/newsroom/press-releases/climate-impact-x-completes-first-multi-party-carbon-futures-delivery-as-eligible-custodian-for-abaxx-exchange)

Nodal removal and nature contracts normally stop trading three business days before the last business day of M, **four before that day in December**, and deliver three business days afterward. Older individual factsheets omit the December exception; current Appendix A controls. [Current Appendix A](https://www.nodalexchange.com/wp-content/uploads/nodal-exchange-contract-specifications-rulebook-appendix-a.pdf)

Delivery and credit validity are different obligations. ICE excludes specified exchange and clearing-house liabilities for post-transfer credit validity and carbon reversal. Abaxx requires relevant corresponding-adjustment eligibility but does not require the host country to have completed the accounting adjustment by delivery. These terms belong to the specific contract; neither registry delivery nor clearing creates a universal guarantee of permanent climate benefit. [ICE 7O1.6](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/contractregs/213_SECTION_7O1.pdf), [Abaxx 1405–1406](https://abexcws.directus.app/assets/bbc2693a-8fc5-44c3-92c2-8390ea49f708)

The May 2026 delivery guide accepts all ACCU methods and all NZU types. LGCs must have been created **before the expiry year**: January 2027 accepts eligible certificates created in 2026 or earlier. Across the three products, sellers transfer units by the second business day after last trade; buyer payment and onward delivery complete on the third. Australia and New Zealand use their respective local time conventions. [ASX delivery guide](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/markets/trade-our-derivatives-market/derivatives-market-overview/asx-environmental-futures-delivery-guide.pdf)

Two source details need care. ASX's web summary incorrectly labels LGC quotation in tonnes; the governing contract specifies certificates, an AUD price and settlement price multiplied by 1,000. The current rules also permit exceptional cash settlement when specified circumstances permanently prevent registry delivery. That remedy does not turn these into ordinary cash-settled futures. [ASX Schedule 1, §§2.67.1–2.67.3, dated 21 August 2026](https://www2.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-24-operating-rules/rules/asx-24-operating-rules-schedule.pdf)

ACCU registry terminology also needs versioning: the regulator now manages ANREU accounts within its **Unit and Certificate Registry**. An old registry platform name should not be hardcoded as the enduring identity of the deliverable. [Clean Energy Regulator guidance](https://cer.gov.au/online-systems/new-unit-and-certificate-registry/unit-and-certificate-registry-guidance)

The RVO assessment is published in **cents per U.S. gallon**, while the futures price above is expressed in **USD/gallon**: the numerical assessment needs a division by 100. Quote scale is therefore separate from lot size. [ICE RVO specification](https://www.ice.com/products/82270937)

ICE also lists daily and balance-of-month RVO futures. The daily contract's last trade is the second business day after its nominal contract date, another reason not to treat every futures label as an expiry month. [Daily RVO](https://www.ice.com/products/82471466/Biofuel-Outright---Daily-Argus-RVO-Current-Year-Future), [balance-of-month RVO](https://www.ice.com/products/82471473/Biofuel-Outright-Argus-RVO-Current-Year-Balmo-Future)

Massachusetts Class I RECs illustrate a separate registry and vintage axis: delivery uses NEPOOL GIS, and ICE lists **May 2027 / vintage 2026** series. [Massachusetts specification](https://www.ice.com/products/65898881/Massachusetts-Compliance-Renewable-Energy-Certificate-Class-I-Future/specs), [expiry series](https://www.ice.com/products/65898881/Massachusetts-Compliance-Renewable-Energy-Certificate-Class-I-Future/expiry)

Nodal's catalogue adds bankable RECs, state-specific and multi-state certificates, Clean Peak, alternative-energy and emission-free certificates, and renewable-natural-gas certificates. Those are additional product-definition families, not evidence that a power load shape belongs on every certificate trade. Current NOx listings include **CSAPR Ozone Season Group 2 and Group 3**. These are catalogue-level inclusions here; detailed current NOx terms and current SO2 availability were not established. [Nodal environmental catalogue](https://www.nodalexchange.com/products-services/environmental/)

## Cash index and other structural boundaries

**ICE Global Carbon Index Futures — CO2** settles in cash to the official closing **excess-return** index, ICECRBN. Its multiplier is **USD 50 per index point**, with a 0.20-point tick worth USD 10. March/June/September/December contracts stop trading on the last business day of the preceding month, adjusted backward if the index is not calculated that day. There is no fixed deliverable tonnage. [ICE contract specification](https://www.ice.com/products/82118761/Global-Carbon-Index-Futures)

The index family currently covers EUA, CCA, RGGI and UKA futures and publishes excess-return, total-return and spot-return variants. The future's reference must identify the exact variant and methodology; substituting the spot-return series would change its economics. Components, weights, rolls and currency treatment belong to the referenced index definition. [ICE carbon index family](https://www.ice.com/fixed-income-data-services/index-solutions/commodity-indices/carbon-futures)

**ICE NGX Alberta environmental futures** also belong in scope: NGX explicitly offers exchange trading and clearing of physically settled environmental futures. AEPCs are performance credits; AEOs are offsets or eligible sequestration credits. Each lot contains **1,000 credits**, quoted in CAD/credit with a published one-cent convention. Eligibility uses remaining usable life relative to the named vintage; AEO additionally excludes specified quantification protocols. [NGX market description](https://www.ice.com/ngx), [AEPC specification](https://www.ice.com/products/83047169/Alberta-Emission-Performance-Credit-Vintage-2024-Futures), [AEO specification](https://www.ice.com/products/83047159/Alberta-Emission-Offset-Vintage-2023-Futures)

NGX last trade is the last business day of expiry month. In the following settlement month, seller delivery is due by the fifth business day, buyer delivery by the tenth, and final payment is stated as the twentieth calendar day. These specifications use the original transaction **Fixed Price** for final settlement. Full daily-margin cashflows and holiday adjustment of the stated payment date remain to be verified before implementation. [AEPC terms](https://www.ice.com/products/83047169/Alberta-Emission-Performance-Credit-Vintage-2024-Futures), [AEO terms](https://www.ice.com/products/83047159/Alberta-Emission-Offset-Vintage-2023-Futures)

Japan's TSE carbon-credit market and its OTC settlement service do not by themselves establish a futures listing. This review did not establish current exchange-futures specifications for domestic Chinese, Japanese or Korean carbon units; they are coverage gaps rather than invented product rows. [JPX carbon-credit market](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/carbon-credit/index.html)

## What this adds to the general futures template

These are design implications drawn from the contracts above. They describe facts the eventual representation must preserve; they do not prescribe a large constructor with every field populated on every trade.

| Contract fact | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Asset identity** | Scheme, jurisdiction, unit type and issuing program identify the economic asset. EUA, EUA2, UKA, a project credit and a renewable certificate cannot share one generic carbon identifier. |
| **Quantity and quote dimensions** | Retain lot size, unit, currency, quote scale, multiplier and tick separately. Metric tonnes, short tons, MWh certificates, RINs and index points have different meanings. |
| **Eligible delivery basket** | An allowance may accept earlier vintages; another contract requires one named year. A credit basket can restrict registry, program, project method, geography, labels and compliance authorization. Certificate baskets can require several state qualifications at once. |
| **Independent time concepts** | Futures maturity, named vintage, generation/reduction period, issuance date, compliance phase and certificate expiration cannot be collapsed into one year. Some products have no vintage restriction. |
| **Reference-price specification** | Cash settlement can use an assessment average, a weighted compliance basket, a closing index level or another future. Record publisher, exact series, observation period, aggregation and currency/units. |
| **Expiry outcome** | Distinguish a registry asset transfer, cash settlement and conversion into a specified futures position. A general physical/cash flag is insufficient if it is the only lifecycle description. |
| **Delivery and payment events** | Notice, seller transfer, buyer acceptance, cash payment and final completion can occur on different days with different calendars and time zones. |
| **Conditions and remedies** | Price caps, invalidation provisions, replacement duties, registry disruption and exceptional cash remedies are contract-specific. Do not infer a universal carbon-credit warranty from physical delivery. |
| **Version and listing status** | Preserve the rules and eligibility applicable to the trade/series. A currently accessible product page can describe a suspended or delisted contract. |

For example, a **December 2027 future referencing a 2026 vintage** has at least two independent dates. Its basket might accept only 2026 units, all eligible units through 2026, or a rolling generation window. Those are different obligations even when the lot and quotation match. The eligibility rule belongs to the product definition; the actual serial numbers and registry account movements arise when delivery is allocated.

An electricity REC future can have a 1,000 MWh certificate lot without any hourly load shape or power-delivery schedule. A carbon-index future can have exposure to several allowance markets without an obligation to receive any of their units. These examples argue for composing the shared futures facts with the applicable settlement terms, rather than assuming every environmental contract inherits the physical mechanics of the related energy commodity.

The ordinary linear exposure still uses signed contracts and a monetary multiplier against a change in the relevant quoted futures price. That observation does not settle valuation before expiry, delivery-basket optionality, financing or the consequences of a conversion event. Following the library's contract/data separation, curve choice, calibration and pricing algorithms remain outside the trade's economic facts.

## Coverage and implementation limits

This is a representative structural survey, not a security master of every state REC, vintage, compliance phase or expiry. The linked exchange specifications provide the starting point for individual product definitions. Exact holiday calendars, delivery-account eligibility, effective amendments and live series must be resolved when adding a product.

Several familiar names require explicit status treatment:

- **EEX standalone EUAA aviation futures** were delisted effective 12 May 2025. Eligible EUAAs can still enter EUA delivery; that does not establish a current standalone aviation future. [EEX notice](https://www.eex.com/fileadmin/Global/News/EEX/EEX_Customer_Information/2025/20250428_EEX_CI_EU_ETS2_Futures_final.pdf)
- **CME CCA specific vintages 2021–2023** were delisted 16 March 2026; a replacement current CME CCA listing was not established here. [CME filing 26-105](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/market-regulation/rule-filings/2026/3/26-105.pdf)
- **CME C-GEO** was permanently delisted 2 January 2025. Its old Core terminology does not establish a current future on CCP-labelled credits; CBL spot products are separate. [CME SER 9420](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/notices/ser/2024/08/ser-9420.pdf)
- **ICE NBT and OVA–OVJ**, eleven nature-based vintage-basket futures, were suspended immediately on 28 August 2026. Cessation of admission was scheduled for 11 September subject to governance/regulatory completion; they are absent from the current catalogue, but a separate completion notice was not found. They are excluded from the available-contract table. [ICE circular 26/128](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/circulars/26128.pdf), [current emissions catalogue](https://www.ice.com/products/Futures-Options/Energy/Emissions)
- **Nodal/IncubEx GER basket** remains in the product listing. Its earlier specification delivered a weighted combination of Base, Forestry, Prime and Carbon Capture subcontracts. Applicable September 2026 weights were not verified; dated weights should not be carried forward. [Current product listing](https://theincubex.com/north-american-products/), [earlier exchange specification](https://www.nodalexchange.com/wp-content/uploads/Appendix-A-REDLINE.pdf)
- Additional state REC and RIN vintages, GO technology baskets and Nodal certificates are catalogue coverage where noted, not fully transcribed specifications. Historical SO2/NOx filings were not substituted for verified current terms. [Nodal rulebook resources](https://www.nodalexchange.com/regulation/nodal-exchange-regulation/participant-agreement-and-rulebook/)

Primary source precedence for implementation should be the governing exchange rules and effective notices, followed by the applicable clearing/delivery rules, then product summaries. Where public sources conflict, the difference is recorded rather than silently normalized.
