# Chemicals and industrial materials futures: contract terms for a general trade representation

Reviewed 14 September 2026. This survey covers exchange-listed futures on chemicals, polymers, manufactured fibres, pulp, paper and related industrial materials. Its purpose is to identify contractual structures for the eventual futures trade template. It does not propose implementation or treat every product in an exchange catalogue as liquid or presently available in every maturity.

The important distinction is between **a chemical price reference** and **a deliverable manufactured product**. A methanol futures contract may settle against a regional price assessment, or transfer warehouse rights to qualifying methanol. In the latter case, composition, tax status, storage, transport qualifications and delivery documents matter. For polymers and paper, manufacturer, physical form, packaging and production dates can also determine eligibility.

The main numerical examples below come from CME/NYMEX, SGX, Zhengzhou Commodity Exchange (ZCE/CZCE) and Shanghai Futures Exchange (SHFE). Dalian Commodity Exchange (DCE) belongs in this universe, but retrieval of its current operative chemical contract rules was incomplete; its coverage is explicitly separated below. INE natural rubber belongs with the existing rubber survey rather than being counted again here.

## Groups and boundaries

| Group | Representative scope | Distinction to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Alcohols and chemical feedstocks | Methanol; polymer-grade propylene | Substance, regional basis, purity or named assessment; physical versus cash. |
| Aromatics and intermediates | Benzene, paraxylene, styrene, monoethylene glycol | FOB/CFR reference location; outright versus separately listed processing spread. |
| Polyolefins and vinyls | LLDPE, HDPE, polypropylene, PVC | Resin family alone is insufficient: film, blow-moulding and raffia references differ. |
| Polyester chain | PX, PTA, MEG, polyester staple fibre, bottle-grade PET chips | Distinct products along one production chain; grade and packaging conditions change downstream. |
| Inorganic chemicals and construction materials | Soda ash, caustic soda, flat glass | Dry-content accounting, hazardous-liquid delivery and dimensional quantity conversion. |
| Forest and elastomer materials | Woodpulp, offset paper, synthetic rubber | Air-dry mass, branded production, roll/bale specifications and warrant expiry. |

Lumber and representative natural-rubber contracts are covered in [agriculture](agricultural-futures-contracts.md); INE TSR20 remains an extension in that family. Industrial silicon, lithium carbonate, alumina, ferroalloys and other metals are in [metals](metals-futures-contracts.md). Petroleum feedstocks, naphtha and blending components are in [petroleum](petroleum-futures-contracts.md); LPG and gas feedstocks are in [gas/NGL](natural-gas-and-ngl-futures-contracts.md). Fertilizer products, including urea and ammonia, are a separate survey. A commercial conversion margin assembled from several futures is not automatically a separately listed spread future.

`M` denotes the futures contract month; `t` means metric tonne, `lb` pound, `TD` exchange trading day and `BD` the business-day calendar specified by the rule. A tick is the quoted-price increment; the parenthesized amount is the calculated monetary tick per lot. Calendar days, trading days and publication days are deliberately distinguished. Unless a row states otherwise, the exchange's general clearing rules still govern payment mechanics beyond the product chapter.

These examples require more than `monthly_average = true`. Preserve the publisher's observation schedule, averaging window, last trading day, price determination and cash-payment event separately. Each event needs its own holiday adjustment. A selected BALMO start date is part of the contract's economic identity.

For illustration, the Houston rule produces an inclusive 27 August–25 September pricing window for September 2026. A 0.25-cent move multiplied by 42,000 gallons is USD 105, after converting cents to dollars. That illustrates why quotation scale belongs beside currency and physical unit, even when a chapter expresses contract value as quantity multiplied by price. [Houston rule](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/NYMEX/9/985.pdf).

The caustic conversion is a contractual normalization, not an instruction to multiply by whatever concentration an assay happens to report. Its 30 dry-tonne delivery unit corresponds to 93.75 wet tonnes using the base factor, or 60 wet tonnes using the substitute factor. The table's different grade and location adjustments remain applicable. [Caustic rules](https://www.czce.com.cn/cn/uploadfile/2024/11/05/20241105170901759.pdf).

Tax status also affects cash mechanics. Under the cited methanol rules, the bonded settlement price removes relevant fees, import VAT, specific consumption tax and ad-valorem customs duty in the prescribed order. The buyer funds the required balance before 09:00 on delivery day three; customs processing and invoice deadlines follow separately. A generic `tax_included` flag cannot capture that sequence or the distinction between the quoted benchmark and the delivery invoice. Current tax rates and any revised formula must be retrieved separately. [Methanol rules, articles 48–52](https://www.czce.com.cn/cn/uploadfile/2024/02/07/20240207154824122.pdf).

- **SGX broader petrochemicals:** the retrieved futures rules identify Platts Benzene FOB Korea, PX CFR China, Methanol CFR China, Benzene–Naphtha and Paraxylene–Naphtha futures. These merit follow-up for complete lot and formula terms. A separately listed spread needs its own component assessments, signs, quantities and observation conventions. SGX historical material also discusses polyethylene, polypropylene and MEG, but mixed swap/futures language is insufficient to import every associated product as a current future. [Current futures rules](https://rulebook.sgx.com/rulebook/futures-trading-rules), [tick appendix](https://rulebook.sgx.com/rulebook/appendix-b-regulatory-notice-4111).
- **CME variants:** the current index retains HDPE, LLDPE and PP BALMO chapters. Only the LLDPE BALMO chapter was transcribed here, with its inconsistency exposed. Do not copy its 47,000 lb lot to monthly polypropylene: the latter explicitly specifies 100,000 lb. Other publisher, region or time-window variants need their own rules. [NYMEX index](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/NYMEX/).
- **Remaining industrial materials:** DCE fibreboard/blockboard, other pulp and recovered-paper references, recycling resins and any proposed chemical futures are follow-up candidates, not confirmed current additions. Spot auctions, physical forward contracts, cleared swaps and options remain outside this futures inventory.

The review is a dated specification survey, not a certified archive of every operative exchange amendment. It uses current 2026 rules where retrieved, and identifies older dated texts where those supplied the evidence. PTA and PET PDFs remained searchable on the exchange domain but returned direct-fetch errors; only supported indexed passages were used. The 14 May 2026 PF rules were retrieved directly and supersede older descriptions of factory-only delivery. Chinese controlling rules, product-annex changes, approved brands, delivery warehouses, geographic differentials and maturity-specific notices must be resolved before production use. General clearing cash-payment cutoffs were not comprehensively transcribed; **absence of a payment date here means unverified, not payment at expiry**.

## Implications for the eventual trade template

These are design inferences from the surveyed contracts, not additional exchange terms.

1. **Separate identity from economic exposure.** Store exchange, product and maturity identity, with a versioned specification reference. The commodity taxonomy can connect methanol, styrene or polyester-chain products without asserting that their prices, lots or settlement obligations coincide.
2. **Keep the units explicit.** Quantity needs a measure basis as well as a unit: gallons, pounds, ordinary tonnes, contractual dry tonnes and measured air-dry tonnes are not interchangeable. Quote currency and quote scale must survive normalization. Conversion provenance should identify whether a factor is contractual, assay-based or dimensional.
3. **Represent settlement as a choice of contractual routes.** A physically deliverable future can permit several warrant or export routes. Distinguish the routes the product allows from the route elected for an actual delivery. Tax status, facility, receipt type and invoice basis may change after that election.
4. **Describe a cash reference precisely.** Publisher, assessment name, region, grade, delivery basis, origin eligibility, range-midpoint extraction and averaging schedule form the reference identity. Observations already published belong to fixing state; missing publications and subsequent corrections need stated rules rather than guessed defaults.
5. **Use multiple dated events.** Last trading, last observation, price determination, warrant transfer, customs clearance, delivery completion, invoice issue and cash payment are different events. Each may use a different calendar and adjustment. A period-average future can remain economically exposed to later fixings after trading ends.
6. **Model physical eligibility as versioned reference data.** Chemical purity limits, fibre dimensions, paper grammage, approved brands, package sizes, age limits and inspection methods belong with the contract's deliverable rules. A named quality standard can have exceptions; storing only its document number loses those exceptions.
7. **Separate nominal size from delivered size.** Trading lots, delivery multiples, actual weighed quantity, standard-warrant quantity, tolerances and inspection batches are different facts. Preserve who chooses a permitted substitute or tolerance and how the resulting invoice is adjusted.
8. **Keep operational restrictions out of the payoff formula.** Delivery eligibility, position limits, margin schedules and handling credentials constrain lifecycle actions. They are important reference data but do not turn an otherwise linear price exposure into a new payoff family.

For this family, a useful first template is therefore a standard futures trade pointing to a sufficiently rich contract specification, with either a precise cash-reference definition or physical-delivery rules. The survey argues for shared units, calendars, references and delivery concepts across commodities, while allowing chemical-specific eligibility and measurement details to remain product data.
