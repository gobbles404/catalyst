# Dated windows in quotes and requests for quote

**Status:** Agreed 1 November 2026 as issue #235, two pull requests stacked on #234, the arc one stack: the first moves the window's shared rules to owners a booking and a quote share and adds `_QuoteByDates`, a quote by two dates beside `_Quote`, which is unchanged, this pull request; the second gives `_CreateRFQ` a ticket with dated rows, the `period` readback and the migration. [quote-by-dates.md](../../library/quoting/quote-by-dates.md), [quote.md](../../library/quoting/quote.md), [rfq.md](../../library/quoting/rfq.md) and [quote-template.md](../../library/quoting/quote-template.md) own the functions; [kernel.md](../../library/trades/kernel.md) the rules they reuse.

## The issue

Complete the quote-to-book workflow for dated whole-period futures Asian swaps. Booking support is implemented in #231 and the stacked PRs #232, #233 and #234; this work builds on that series.

Today, `_Quote` accepts contract/strip/balance labels and constructs monthly components. `_CreateRFQ` accepts one contract month per row; its optional dates are payment dates, not observation boundaries. Shared quote terms explicitly reject `onetime` and `period`, although quote templates can hold those values. A user can therefore book and value a TMA window but cannot quote the same dated payoff through these functions.

**Required behavior**

- Add explicit `start_date` / `end_date` entry to `_Quote` and to each `_CreateRFQ` row. A complete date pair must work without a dummy contract code. Where a code and dates coexist, follow booking: both dates determine the period and bypass code parsing; one date alone fails; neither date preserves the existing label path. Preserve direct quotes' strip/BAL inputs and RFQs' existing monthly-code input.
- Keep `settlement_frequency` required. Support `onetime` with `schedule_method = asian`, financial settlement, one futures index, and `quantity_per = period | calday`, on cleared and bilateral routes. Preserve existing monthly combinations and economics.
- Resolve one calculation period across the inclusive date bounds. Average every eligible pricing day with equal original weights, using the same holiday calendar and nearby-contract selection as booking. Cross-month and cross-expiry windows must not be split into separate monthly averages.
- `period` means the entered total quantity; `calday` means daily quantity multiplied by inclusive calendar days, including weekends and holidays. Quantity basis does not change the fixing weights. For a single-window direct quote, the common quantity scale cancels.
- Support the existing explicit payment date and payment-rule forms. Under `onetime`, a rule such as `+2bd` is anchored to the window's final observation. Preserve RFQ row payment-date overrides and the requirement to pay after the final observation.
- Each onetime RFQ row is its own averaging period. Across rows, retain the existing quantity/payment-discount weighting: `K = sum(Q_i * DF_i * P_i) / sum(Q_i * DF_i)`. Do not pool all rows' observation days into one average or average row prices equally. Preserve RFQ quantity restrictions: finite, nonnegative quantities, with at least one positive.
- Explicit observation dates stay fixed when the market's valuation date changes. Partial fixings retain the original window and weights; a full-period request never silently becomes a balance request. Preserve the existing full-period convention for elapsed payments, including their factor of one.
- Identify onetime periods by their dates in RFQ readbacks and refusals. Show the entered quantity/basis, resolved period quantity and payment date. Do not require an unrelated contract for the calendar month in which the window begins.

**Implementation direction**

Reuse shared booking rules for period resolution, observation dates/weights, pricing references, quantities, payment anchors and compatible combinations. Extract a shared owner where necessary rather than maintaining a second set of window rules in quoting.

Reuse the existing quote pricing calculation. Extend the resolved request to represent whole periods; `_CreateRFQ` resolves and retains its static facts once, and `_QuoteRFQ` reprices them without rebuilding dates or observations on every tick. Removing the current refusal alone is insufficient: the current input and request-building paths still assume monthly contracts.

Keep period entry on the quote/request, with static terms in the quote template. Choose the concrete argument/table layout during implementation. If signatures or readback shapes change, migrate current callers, workbook examples, host facts and smoke tests together; no compatibility shim.

**Acceptance**

- [ ] Direct quote and a one-row RFQ agree for the same dates, terms, market and payment.
- [ ] WTI regression from #231/#234: 26 May–25 June 2026, 22 observations, 19 on N26 and three on Q26, paying 29 June. With July at 70 and August at 72 before fixing, both quote `(19*70 + 3*72)/22 = 70.272727...`.
- [ ] The same window works without K26 in the source schedule. References remain N26/Q26. Weekend/holiday boundaries and uncovered pricing dates follow booking.
- [ ] On 10 June, with eleven observations fixed at 69 and the remaining eight July observations at 70 and three August observations at 72, both quote `(11*69 + 8*70 + 3*72)/22 = 69.772727...`. Original weights are retained; corrected fixings update the result.
- [ ] `calday = 100` gives 3,100 barrels over that 31-day window and the same single-window price as `period = 3100`.
- [ ] Booking equivalent quantities/windows/payments at the quoted fixed price gives zero gross mark while every payment is still ahead: cleared and bilateral, before fixing, partly fixed, and fully fixed but unpaid. Fees/reserves remain separate. Do not apply this zero-mark assertion to requests with elapsed payments; preserve their existing quote convention.
- [ ] Multiple RFQ windows with unequal quantities, observation counts and payment dates match the quantity/discount-weighted reference and the equivalent booked portfolio. Include repeated windows and row reordering.
- [ ] Explicit payment dates and equivalent rules agree; invalid dates, incomplete date pairs, missing market/curve coverage and unsupported combinations fail clearly.
- [ ] Existing monthly quote/RFQ results, strip/BAL behavior and payment overrides remain unchanged.
- [ ] Cover the worksheet/host path, RFQ readbacks and retained-handle reuse. Update WorkCounter/performance budgets and report before/after counts, including no period/observation reconstruction when repricing a saved RFQ. Update owning documentation and kept workbook examples; run the full `check.ps1`.

**Outside this issue**

No new TMA market or futures calendar, automatic named-TMA date generator, two-index onetime spreads, physical onetime settlement, IFERC/Gas Daily support, new averaging/option models, or automatic strip-final payment anchoring. A monthly NG strip paid on one common explicit date remains distinct from one Asian average over the whole strip.

## The review and the decisions

The review of 1 November 2026 ran the issue's WTI example through the tree of #234. The number the issue asks for is already fixed by booking: the window of 26 May to 25 June 2026 booked at `(19 * 70 + 3 * 72) / 22`, 70.272727, marks 0.000000000 on 20 May with 1,900 barrels of delta to N26 and 300 to Q26. `_Quote` and `_CreateRFQ` refused a `onetime` template by name, `settlement_frequency is 'onetime', expected 'monthly'; a quote prices by the month and does not price a onetime period across a window's dates as monthly`; `_Quote`'s period is a label alone, `K26` quoting the whole of May at 70, and the request's date column is payment dates, so the window could be entered on neither surface. What the window needs is shared since #234: the frequency's rules, the one component, the contract pricing its first day, the asian weights, the payment anchored on the final observation and `calday` times inclusive days; the first-day contract search and the period's words were private to the resolution and move to a shared owner, and the frequency's rules take the words that name a quote or a request row in place of a trade's. The seam decides the entry: it has no scalar that is text or a date, a blank cell in a column fails, and only a left-out argument takes a default, so a dated quote enters through a function of its own with two required dates, `_QuoteByDates`, `_Quote` keeping its labels as they are, and a dated request through a ticket's rows.

The owner's decisions, 1 November 2026:

1. **Entry.** A function of its own for dates, `_QuoteByDates(market, start_date, end_date, template, [discount_curve_set])`, beside `_Quote(market, contract_code, template, [discount_curve_set])`, which is unchanged: `_Quote` keeps the contract, strip and balance labels with their meaning, `_QuoteByDates` takes explicit dates, the trade-month window among them, every dated input required and no placeholder argument, and no existing formula migrates. The name says how the period is supplied and stays true under any settlement frequency; no `CFD`, `TMA` or `Asian` in it, those name a contract's economics and the function's one distinction is date entry. Both feed one period resolution and one pricing engine, as `_QuoteRFQ` does for a saved request, and a frequency added later leaves the name and the signature alone. Each input keeps its own duty: the two dates give the inclusive period; `schedule_method` which prices are observed; `settlement_frequency` separate monthly calculations or one across the window; `quantity_per` how the quantity applies; `payment_day` when each amount pays. So the same dates under two frequencies are two quotes. Over 26 May to 25 June 2026 with N26 at 70 and Q26 at 72, cleared: `monthly` by `month` 70.166667, the four May days at 70 and the eighteen June days at 70.333333 in equal quantities; `monthly` by `calday` 70.268817, the two portions weighted 6 and 25; `onetime` by `period` or `calday` 70.272727, one average of 22 fixings. Each monthly portion pays from its own final observation when the payment is a rule; an explicit common date pays both together and keeps the two averages apart. A bilateral monthly quote weights each portion by its payment's discount factor as well; the numbers assume cleared or equal factors. `_CreateRFQ(index, headers, rows, template)` takes a ticket as `_CreateTrades` does, each row choosing its period by booking's rule: both dates book the days between them and a code beside them is kept as text; one date alone fails; neither books the code.
2. **One frequency per request.** `settlement_frequency` and `quantity_per` stay in the quote template. Code rows and dated rows stand together in one request; every row follows the template's calculation frequency. Mixing `monthly` and `onetime` calculations in one request is outside this issue.
3. **The readback.** The request readback names each row's period under `period`, the month's code or the exact dates. The request's quantity rules stand, finite and zero or more with at least one positive, fractions allowed: reusing booking's period logic imposes none of booking's whole-number rule.
4. **The order of work.** Two pull requests stacked on #234, since the owner's word of 1 November that the arc is one stack: the shared window and `_QuoteByDates` first, the request's ticket entry, readback and migration second, each landing in order after the one beneath it.

Payments keep today's behavior: a row's supplied payment day is taken exactly; with the column absent the template's date or rule pays each row; no blank-cell fallback. A single window quotes the same price cleared and bilateral, its one factor cancelling; the discount weighting matters across request rows paying on different days. The ticket's payment column takes the instrument's own field name, `payment_day`, and holds dates alone for a request, a rule text per row refused by name, the template holding the rule.

## The slices
