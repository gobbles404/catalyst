# _EvaluateTrades

Built in #141 as ticket I5 of the [trade-valuation pipeline](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md), with [_PrepareTrades](prepare-trades.md), [_ReportValues](report-values.md) and [_ReportRisk](report-risk.md); the design is [evaluation](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/04-evaluation.md) and [results](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/05-results.md).

Prepared trades evaluated against one market: one quote per live contract read off the market, a contract's payment groups sharing it, gathered onto the live slots and multiplied by their exposure for the live amount, which the unsettled offsets and set amounts complete into the mark; the cash as the preparation settled it; the risk, the preparation's, shared, the sensitivity by contract in the settlement currency and, since #164, the delta in lots of the index's reporting lot by contract with its total, [lot-delta-reporting.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/lot-delta-reporting.md); DV01, zero by the undiscounted cleared model. One immutable snapshot for the batch as a whole, retained with the dated contributions that explain it. Creating one is the tick: a new market over the same preparation makes new results and does nothing of the preparation or the encoding again, and the old results stand. The kernel's `trade_results` does the work, [kernel.md](kernel.md); the code is `src/goosedove/excel/trades.py`. Since #176 the evaluator values a bilateral batch on a discount curve, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path), and since #177 this function takes the curve, optional and last, [bilateral-excel-integration.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/bilateral-excel-integration.md): a bilateral batch's payments are discounted on it, the mark the unpaid NPV and the cash the nominal paid amounts, both lot deltas and the analytic DV01 stored for the reports; a cleared batch takes none, and one supplied is refused.

Two handles, both required, neither with a default: the prepared trades, the cell showing `_PrepareTrades`'s result or a name for it, and the market over the history they were prepared against, the cell showing `_CreateMarket`'s result or a name for it, with the day's quotes. A third, optional and last since #177, in decision 39's form, [inputs.md](../../seam/inputs.md): the discount curve, the cell showing `_CreateDiscountCurve`'s result or a name for it, for a bilateral batch; left out, the argument is nothing, which a cleared batch wants and a bilateral one refuses, and a blank cell is not left out, `discount_curve is blank, expected discount curve handle`, nor is empty text, a number, `discount_curve is number 1.0, expected discount curve handle`, a dead handle or a handle of another kind, `discount_curve is market handle '30512:market:UNIT:52', expected discount curve handle`. The seam resolves the handles and nothing else; a cell of another kind fails in the seam's words, `prepared is trades handle '30512:trades:Henry Hub NYMEX:41', expected prepared trades handle`. The result is a handle, `30512:trade_results:Henry Hub NYMEX:45`, named for the index. A successful call publishes results and changes neither input; a recalculation with the same arguments shares the live results; a new market makes new results and the old are released when Excel disconnects their last creator, a report referring to the old cell holding them meanwhile.

## The library checks

The kernel's rules, in [kernel.md](kernel.md)'s words after `#ERROR _EvaluateTrades: ValueError:`.

| Rule | Refused as |
|---|---|
| The market is over the history the batch was prepared against, the one object; a later history is a new preparation, never a silent one here | `market is over another history of index 'Henry Hub NYMEX', expected the one the trades were prepared against` |
| Since #176 a bilateral batch is financial, a physical delivery having no payment to discount | `settlement_method is 'physical', expected 'financial' with a bilateral batch; a physical delivery has no payment to discount` |
| Since #176 a bilateral batch is valued on a discount curve, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path), supplied since #177; left out, a bilateral batch is refused asking for it, every payment paid or not | `discount_curve is missing, expected a discount curve with a bilateral batch, whose payments it discounts` |
| A cleared batch takes no curve, since #177: one supplied is refused by name, not ignored | `discount_curve is supplied, expected none unless route = bilateral; a cleared batch is undiscounted` |
| The curve starts on the valuation day, never rebased | `discount_curve starts on 2027-01-05, expected 2027-01-04, the valuation date; a curve is never rebased` |
| The curve covers every unpaid payment day, a fixed-but-unpaid group's included; a batch with every payment paid takes a curve of its day alone | `the payment of F27 on 2029-01-03 is past the discount curve, which runs from 2027-01-04 to 2028-12-31; expected every unpaid payment day covered` |
| Every live contract is quoted, the market's own rule | `requested contract 'F27' is beyond live-price coverage ending at 'Z26'` |

The quotes carry no labels, as [market.md](../market/market.md#when-the-first-contract-rolls) records: the first price is the first unfixed contract's and the rest follow the schedule. A batch prepared again after the valuation date rolls past a contract's expiry takes the first quote for the next contract, so a vector left in place across the roll values the batch's live contracts at the quotes of the contracts before them, and nothing refuses it unless the vector reached the schedule's end, where it is now a price too many. Move the vector with the date, as the lifetime's `valuation` group does on a sheet after walking into the refusal, since #155; labels for the vector are deferred work, recorded under [Accepted constraints](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md#accepted-constraints).

Under an averaging method, `last3day` and `asian`, a live price is the projection of the contract's remaining unfixed days at one common price, and the history supplies the daily prices being averaged: the market performs no conversion from a quote of the whole month's average, which is not that projection once a day has fixed, and the delta is to the projection input, not to such a quote, since #158, [asian-trades.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/asian-trades.md).

## What it retains

The preparation and the market by reference; the route, the currency and the valuation day; the mark and the cash; per live slot its dated contribution, exposure times its contract's quote, allocating neither the offsets nor the mark among dates; the batch's contract axis with the preparation's sensitivity, its delta in lots and the lots' total, shared and never written, each with its unit; DV01; and since #170 the batch's reserves, the encoding's record by reference, booked when the batch was made and in no number of value or risk. Since #176, for a bilateral batch, the curve, the discounted lot delta by reference and its total, the discounted sensitivity, the discounted lots by day, month and year, and per payment group the amount, the NPV, the DV01 and the factor; for a cleared batch the discounted arrays are the nominal ones by reference and the group vectors none.
