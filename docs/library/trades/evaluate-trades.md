# _EvaluateTrades

Built in #141 as ticket I5 of the [trade-valuation pipeline](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md), with [_PrepareTrades](prepare-trades.md), [_ReportValues](report-values.md) and [_ReportRisk](report-risk.md); the design is [evaluation](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/04-evaluation.md) and [results](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/05-results.md).

Prepared trades evaluated against one market: one quote per live contract read off the market, a contract's payment groups sharing it, gathered onto the live slots and multiplied by their exposure for the live amount, which the unsettled offsets and set amounts complete into the mark; the cash as the preparation settled it; the risk, the preparation's, shared, the sensitivity by contract in the settlement currency and, since #164, the delta in lots of the index's reporting lot by contract with its total, [lot-delta-reporting.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/lot-delta-reporting.md); DV01, zero by the undiscounted cleared model. One immutable snapshot for the batch as a whole, retained with the dated contributions that explain it. Creating one is the tick: a new market over the same preparation makes new results and does nothing of the preparation or the encoding again, and the old results stand. The kernel's `trade_results` does the work, [kernel.md](kernel.md); the code is `src/goosedove/excel/trades.py`. Since #176 the evaluator behind this function values a bilateral batch on a discount curve, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path), privately: the function takes no curve yet, so a bilateral batch is refused here asking for one, until the next slice adds the argument and the readers' columns.

Two handles, both required, neither with a default: the prepared trades, the cell showing `_PrepareTrades`'s result or a name for it, and the market over the history they were prepared against, the cell showing `_CreateMarket`'s result or a name for it, with the day's quotes. The seam resolves the two handles and nothing else; a cell of another kind fails in the seam's words, `prepared is trades handle '30512:trades:Henry Hub NYMEX:41', expected prepared trades handle`. The result is a handle, `30512:trade_results:Henry Hub NYMEX:45`, named for the index. A successful call publishes results and changes neither input; a recalculation with the same arguments shares the live results; a new market makes new results and the old are released when Excel disconnects their last creator, a report referring to the old cell holding them meanwhile.

## The library checks

The kernel's rules, in [kernel.md](kernel.md)'s words after `#ERROR _EvaluateTrades: ValueError:`.

| Rule | Refused as |
|---|---|
| The market is over the history the batch was prepared against, the one object; a later history is a new preparation, never a silent one here | `market is over another history of index 'Henry Hub NYMEX', expected the one the trades were prepared against` |
| Since #176 a bilateral batch is financial, a physical delivery having no payment to discount | `settlement_method is 'physical', expected 'financial' with a bilateral batch; a physical delivery has no payment to discount` |
| Since #176 a bilateral batch is valued on a discount curve, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path); this function passes none until the next slice adds the argument, so a bilateral batch is refused here asking for it | `discount_curve is missing, expected a discount curve with a bilateral batch, whose payments it discounts` |
| Every live contract is quoted, the market's own rule | `requested contract 'F27' is beyond live-price coverage ending at 'Z26'` |

The quotes carry no labels, as [market.md](../market/market.md#when-the-first-contract-rolls) records: the first price is the first unfixed contract's and the rest follow the schedule. A batch prepared again after the valuation date rolls past a contract's expiry takes the first quote for the next contract, so a vector left in place across the roll values the batch's live contracts at the quotes of the contracts before them, and nothing refuses it unless the vector reached the schedule's end, where it is now a price too many. Move the vector with the date, as the lifetime's `valuation` group does on a sheet after walking into the refusal, since #155; labels for the vector are deferred work, recorded under [Accepted constraints](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md#accepted-constraints).

Under an averaging method, `last3day` and `asian`, a live price is the projection of the contract's remaining unfixed days at one common price, and the history supplies the daily prices being averaged: the market performs no conversion from a quote of the whole month's average, which is not that projection once a day has fixed, and the delta is to the projection input, not to such a quote, since #158, [asian-trades.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/asian-trades.md).

For the NG example on 24 November 2026 at 3.20 and 3.50: a mark of 7,000 USD, no cash, a sensitivity of 160,000 and -50,000 USD per USD/MMBTU and a delta of 16 and -5 lots of NYMEX NG (10000 MMBTU), total 11; at 3.21 the mark is 8,600 and the risk unchanged. For the toy on 30 November, Z26 fixed at 3.23 and paid, F27 at 3.50: a mark of -11,000 and cash of 22,800; its seven states are the example page's table, and [kernel.md](kernel.md) has them with the quotes and fixings read.

## What it retains

The preparation and the market by reference; the route, the currency and the valuation day; the mark and the cash; per live slot its dated contribution, exposure times its contract's quote, allocating neither the offsets nor the mark among dates; the batch's contract axis with the preparation's sensitivity, its delta in lots and the lots' total, shared and never written, each with its unit; DV01; and since #170 the batch's reserves, the encoding's record by reference, booked when the batch was made and in no number of value or risk. Since #176, for a bilateral batch, the curve, the discounted lot delta by reference and its total, the discounted sensitivity, the discounted lots by day, month and year, and per payment group the amount, the NPV, the DV01 and the factor; for a cleared batch the discounted arrays are the nominal ones by reference and the group vectors none.

## Reading it back

The two reports spill the scalars, [report-values.md](report-values.md) and [report-risk.md](report-risk.md); since #166 [report-delta.md](report-delta.md) spills the delta by observation day, calendar month or calendar year from the three vectors the preparation stored, the selector choosing and nothing summed again; since #170 [report-reserves.md](report-reserves.md) spills the batch's booked brokerage, clearing and marketing with their currency. `util_ReadHandle` spills the dated contents, one row per slot: `pricing_contract`, the contract pricing the day, and `booked_contract`, the one the slot's payment group was booked on, apart since #165 since an `asian` slot may be priced by a contract other than the one it was booked on, [calendar-driven-asian-references.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/calendar-driven-asian-references.md), `observation_date`, for a financial settlement `payment_date`, the day the slot's payment group pays, since #162, `is_fixed`, `live_coefficient`, `live_lots` and `live_contribution`, the slot's own, zero where fixed, the coefficient the settlement currency its price moves the mark by and the lots the same in lots of the index's reporting lot since #164, and `contract_sensitivity` and `contract_lot_delta`, the contract's two numbers read by each slot's reference and so the same on every slot of one contract, whatever group holds it, named for whose they are, and `sensitivity_unit` and `lot_unit`, the units those two numbers are in, the batch's on every row so that a row reads whole and 420 under `USD per CENT/GAL` is never read as the 42,000 under `USD per USD/GAL`: in the daily toy after 1 December fixes the slots show 0, 10 and 10 of coefficient and of lots, the toy's lot a unit, beside 20 of contract sensitivity and 20 lots on each. The NG example spills Z26 with 160,000 of coefficient, 16 lots, 512,000 of contribution, 160,000 of sensitivity and 16 of lot delta, and F27 with -50,000, -5, -175,000, -50,000 and -5, every row carrying `USD per USD/MMBTU` and `lots of NYMEX NG (10000 MMBTU)`. Since #170 four columns end the spill, `brokerage_reserve`, `clearing_reserve`, `marketing_reserve` and `currency`, the batch's reserves once, on the first slot row, as [_ReportReserves](report-reserves.md) spills them, and a dash on every later row, since the totals are the batch's and no slot's: the NG example's first row ends 0, 0, 0 and USD, its rates being free.
