# _EvaluateTrades

Built in #141 as ticket I5 of the [trade-valuation pipeline](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md), with [_PrepareTrades](prepare-trades.md), [_ReportValues](report-values.md) and [_ReportRisk](report-risk.md); the design is [evaluation](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/04-evaluation.md) and [results](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/05-results.md).

Prepared trades evaluated against their markets, one per index: one quote per live contract read off the market, a contract's payment groups sharing it, gathered onto the live slots and multiplied by their exposure for the live amount, which the unsettled offsets and set amounts complete into the mark; the cash as the preparation settled it; the risk, the preparation's, shared, the sensitivity by contract in the settlement currency and, since #164, the delta in lots of the index's reporting lot by contract with its total, [lot-delta-reporting.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/lot-delta-reporting.md); DV01, zero by the undiscounted cleared model. One immutable snapshot for the batch as a whole, retained with the dated contributions that explain it. Creating one is the tick: a new market over the same preparation makes new results and does nothing of the preparation or the encoding again, and the old results stand. The kernel's `trade_results` does the work, [kernel.md](kernel.md); the code is `src/goosedove/excel/trades.py`. Since #176 the evaluator values a bilateral batch on a discount curve, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path), and since #177 this function took the curve, optional and last, [bilateral-excel-integration.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/bilateral-excel-integration.md); since [named-curves-slice-3.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/named-curves-slice-3.md) it takes a collection of named curves in that place, [collect-discount-curves.md](../globals/collect-discount-curves.md), each bilateral row naming its curve by `discount_curve_key` and each payment group discounted on the curve its row named: the mark the unpaid NPV and the cash the nominal paid amounts, the nominal and the discounted delta and the analytic DV01 and cross-gamma per curve stored for the reports; a cleared batch takes none, and one supplied is refused. A bilateral batch stores the cross-gamma beside them, [single-curve-cross-gamma.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/single-curve-cross-gamma.md), the delta's sensitivity to the rate move the DV01 measures, [the cross-gamma](#the-cross-gamma) below.

## Several curves in one batch

## The cross-gamma

The cross-gamma, [single-curve-cross-gamma.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/single-curve-cross-gamma.md), is the sensitivity of the delta to a rise of one basis point in the curve's continuously compounded zero rates, the move the DV01 measures, ACT/365F, in lots of the reporting specification per basis point. Each live slot's discounted lots are its nominal lots times its payment group's factor; a parallel shift of `b` in the rates moves the factor to `D * exp(-b * tau)`, `tau` the calendar days from the valuation day to the payment day over 365, so the derivative at nought is `-tau * D * q` and the slot's cross-gamma is `-0.0001 * tau * q * D`, the payment's own time applied to its own lots before any sum, since two slots of one contract and one day may pay years apart. The slots are summed onto the delta's axes, the references, the observation days and the pricing contract's months and years, with the total, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path); each number is finite or the first that is not refuses the batch by its contract, its bucket or in all. A fixed or a paid slot holds no lots and so no cross-gamma, a paid batch zeros on its axes; a cleared batch holds the known zero, no vector and a total of nothing, since the undiscounted model moves for no rate. No bump and no second evaluation: a quote tick repeats the small arithmetic with the evaluation. The ticket's example, a one-barrel lot settled in dollars on 4 January 2027, A +10 of G27 at 70 and B -5 at 74, both observed on 1 February and quoted 72, A paid on 4 January 2028 at 0.95 and B on 3 January 2029 at 0.90:

| trade | lots | discounted lots | payment | NPV | DV01 | cross-gamma |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 10 | 9.5 | 20 | 19 | -0.0019 | -0.00095 |
| B | -5 | -4.5 | 10 | 9 | -0.0018 | +0.0009 |
| batch | **5** | **5** | **30** | **28** | **-0.0037** | **-0.00005** |

Quoted 73 the NPV is 33 and the DV01 -0.00375 while the deltas and the cross-gamma stand, the cross-gamma no function of the mark; at 0.96 and 0.92 the delta is 5 again, the NPV 28.4, the DV01 -0.00376 and the cross-gamma -0.00004, equal deltas on other factors being other cross-gammas. Both at the live quote the NPV and the DV01 are nothing and the cross-gamma -0.00005 still; +18 and -19 at 70 quoted 80 net 17.1 and -17.1 of discounted lots to nothing and +0.00171 of cross-gamma, each payment's time weighting its own lots before they net. The tests hold the analytic number against a centered difference of the discounted lots on the daily curve shifted by `exp(-h * tau)` and `exp(h * tau)` through the ordinary curve constructor, within the difference's own truncation and roundoff, and the hand figures above to the bilateral kernel's tolerances.
