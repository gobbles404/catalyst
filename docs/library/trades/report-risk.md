# _ReportRisk

Built in #141 as ticket I5 of the [trade-valuation pipeline](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md), with [_ReportValues](report-values.md); the design is [readers](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/06-readers.md) and [result fields by route](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/result-fields-by-route.md). Since #NN the one risk reader, [unified-risk-reporting.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/unified-risk-reporting.md), the first ticket of the [risk reporting and aggregation series](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/aggregate-evaluated-results.md): `_ReportRisk(results, risk, bucket)`, one risk or all three by day, calendar month or calendar year, the saved Total above the dated rows. `_ReportDelta`, built in #166 and widened in #177, is retired by it, its page gone; its views are this function's `nominal_delta` and `delta`, four rows lower.

One risk of the batch from its results, or all three, by time, the saved Total first. `nominal_delta`, since #164, is the batch's exposure still to price counted in lots of the index's reporting specification, by observation day, the preparation's vectors; `delta`, since #177, the same lots weighted by the route's valuation treatment, the nominal lots themselves for a cleared batch and each live slot's lots times its payment group's discount factor for a bilateral one, by observation day, the result's vectors, [bilateral-excel-integration.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/bilateral-excel-integration.md); `dv01`, the change in the mark for a rise of one basis point, by payment day, the day a group's amount is paid, the groups' DV01s summed by the evaluation onto the payment days, their calendar months and their calendar years, axes the encoding made once for a bilateral batch, a paid group's day kept with nothing in it; a cleared batch, whose DV01 is zero because the undiscounted model moves for no rate, has no dated rate axis and reports the Total of nothing alone. `all` is the three side by side on one axis, the union of the observation dates and the payment dates at the level chosen, each vector copied through places the encoding saved and zero where only the other kind has a date, a cleared batch's all on the observation axis with a DV01 column of nothing. The Total row is the scalars the evaluation stored, `lot_delta_total`, `discounted_lot_delta_total` and `dv01`, summed by no one here; the day rows sum to it, the month rows to the days and the year rows to the months, each column on its own. A report values nothing, converts nothing, sorts nothing and bumps nothing. The delta by contract and the sensitivity by contract are the readback's, `util_ReadHandle` on the results, with `lot_unit` and `sensitivity_unit` beside them.

The first cell reads `as_of`, the formula's own cell. Three arguments, every one required and none with a default: the results, the cell showing `_EvaluateTrades`'s result or a name for it; `risk`, exactly `nominal_delta`, `delta`, `dv01` or `all`; `bucket`, exactly `day`, `month` or `year`. A blank, empty text, a number or a left-out selector is refused by the seam before any word is read, and a word that is none of them, `Delta` or `quarter`, by the library naming the four or the three. The spill is four rows and then one per date: a context row, `as_of` and the valuation day with a dash in every other cell; a header row, `period` and one label per risk column; a type row, `str | datetime64[D]` over the period column and `float64` over each risk; the Total row, the word and the saved scalars; then the dates, increasing, each an Excel date to format as the sheet likes, the observation day, the first day of the calendar month or 1 January of the calendar year, a label of the bucket and not an observation, and the saved values beside it. One risk is two columns wide, all three four; a cleared batch's `dv01` is four rows by two, the Total of nothing and no dated row. The example is the kernel's, [kernel.md](kernel.md#the-bilateral-path): +10 and -10 of F27 at 70 observed on 4 January 2027, quoted 80, paying a year and two years out at 0.95 and 0.90. The observation month holds the deltas and the two payment months the DV01s, -0.0095 receivable and +0.018 payable, the Total 0.0085 above them; `=_ReportRisk(A12,"delta","month")` is the Total of 0.5 over the one row of 1 January 2027, acquiring no payment month, and `=_ReportRisk(A12,"dv01","day")` the Total of 0.0085 over 4 January 2028 and 3 January 2029. A quote of 81 moves the DV01s to -0.01045 and +0.0198 and the delta not at all; a curve of 0.96 and 0.92 to -0.0096 and +0.0184 and the delta to 0.4; the axes stand on either tick. A delta label is the index's name, the risk and the reporting lot's name in brackets, `Henry Hub NYMEX nominal_delta [NYMEX NG]` for the NG example, whose eleven lots are its Total under either delta on 24 November 2026, spread over the days each contract observes; the lot's words, `lots of NYMEX NG (10000 MMBTU)`, are the readback's `lot_unit`. A DV01 label is `curve_1`, the local name of the one curve a batch is valued on and no property of the curve, the result's currency, the risk and the currency per basis point in brackets, `curve_1 USD dv01 [USD per basis point]`; a cleared batch's is `cleared USD dv01 [USD per basis point]`. A fixed-but-unpaid group keeps its observation day at zero lots and its payment day with its DV01; a paid group keeps its payment day with nothing in it, a one-day curve enough; every row stands whatever fixes or pays.

## The library checks

| Rule | Refused as |
|---|---|
| The risk is one of the four words, exactly, case and all; a blank, empty text, a number and a left-out argument are the seam's refusals, and no word is a default | `risk is 'gamma', expected nominal_delta, delta, dv01 or all`; `risk is 'Delta', expected nominal_delta, delta, dv01 or all` |
| The bucket is one of the three words, exactly, the same way | `bucket is 'quarter', expected day, month or year`; `bucket is 'Day', expected day, month or year` |

Nothing else: the nominal vectors are complete when the preparation publishes and the discounted vectors, the DV01s and their three views when the evaluation does, each checked finite there, [kernel.md](kernel.md), a view's sum past the largest number refused by its period, `the payment day 2100-01-04 has -inf of DV01 in all, expected a finite amount of USD per basis point; its groups' DV01s sum past the largest number`.

## What it reads

The encoding's observation axes, since #166: the distinct observation days, increasing, the first day of each calendar month those days fall in and the first day of each calendar year, with each slot's day, each day's month and each month's year. Since #NN, for a bilateral financial batch, the payment axes beside them, `PaymentAxes` in `trades/encoding.py`: the distinct payment days of every group, paid or not, their months and their years, three uniques, and at each level the union of the observation axis and the payment axis with each axis's places on it, three unions and six searches, made once when the batch is made and counted as `encoded_payment_days`; a cleared batch, whose DV01 has no dated axis, and a physical one make none. The preparation's three nominal vectors, the live lots summed onto the days, the months and the years, shared by every result over it. The result's three discounted vectors, and since #NN its DV01 by payment day, month and year, the groups' DV01s summed onto the payment axes by three more bincounts, `risk_reductions` 7 where it was 4, each view checked finite and frozen; a cleared result's are None. `risk_view(results, risk, bucket)` in `trades/evaluation.py` is the one way to read a report's content: the axis, the labels, the Totals and the columns, by reference where the axis is the risk's own, through the saved places onto the union under `all`, a copy per column there and nothing anywhere else. `_risk_table` in `excel/trades.py` writes the rectangle by kind, as the readback's once-per-group columns are written, the two date columns through the table builder, which is the check a date result gets.
