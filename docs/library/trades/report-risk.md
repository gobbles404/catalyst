# _ReportRisk

Built in #141 as ticket I5 of the [trade-valuation pipeline](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/README.md), with [_ReportValues](report-values.md); the design is [readers](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/06-readers.md) and [result fields by route](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/result-fields-by-route.md). Since #178 the one risk reader, [unified-risk-reporting.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/unified-risk-reporting.md), the first ticket of the [risk reporting and aggregation series](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/aggregate-evaluated-results.md): `_ReportRisk(results, risk, bucket)`, one risk or all three by day, calendar month or calendar year, the saved Total above the dated rows. `_ReportDelta`, built in #166 and widened in #177, is retired by it, its page gone; its views are this function's `nominal_delta` and `delta`, four rows lower. Since #180, the series' third ticket, [aggregate-risk-excel-integration.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/aggregate-risk-excel-integration.md), the reader takes a collection's handle as well as a batch's, [aggregate-results.md](aggregate-results.md): one declaration of two kinds, the view the resolved object's own. Since #186, [risk-contract-month-bucketing.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/risk-contract-month-bucketing.md), the month and year views of the deltas follow the pricing contract's month and year, the day view the observation day and the DV01 the payment period. A fourth risk, `cross_gamma`, the delta's sensitivity to the rates, [single-curve-cross-gamma.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/single-curve-cross-gamma.md), on the delta's axes under its own selector and appended to `all`.

One risk of the batch from its results, or all four, by time, the saved Total first. `nominal_delta`, since #164, is the batch's exposure still to price counted in lots of the index's reporting specification, by observation day and, since #186, by the calendar month or year of the contract supplying the price, the preparation's vectors; `delta`, since #177, the same lots weighted by the route's valuation treatment, the nominal lots themselves for a cleared batch and each live slot's lots times its payment group's discount factor for a bilateral one, on the same axes, the result's vectors, [bilateral-excel-integration.md](../../engineering-tickets/trade-valuation-pipeline/bilateral-excel-integration.md); `dv01`, the change in the mark for a rise of one basis point, by payment day, the day a group's amount is paid, the groups' DV01s summed by the evaluation onto the payment days, their calendar months and their calendar years, axes the encoding made once for a bilateral batch, a paid group's day kept with nothing in it; a cleared batch, whose DV01 is zero because the undiscounted model moves for no rate, has no dated rate axis and reports the Total of nothing alone. `cross_gamma` is the change in the delta for a rise of one basis point in the curve's continuously compounded zero rates, the move the DV01 measures, in lots of the reporting specification per basis point: each live slot's discounted lots times -0.0001 times its payment's time fraction, summed onto the delta's axes, the observation days and the pricing contract's months and years, the payment supplying the time and never the axis, [evaluate-trades.md](evaluate-trades.md#the-cross-gamma); a cleared batch's is the known zero, a Total of nothing over zeros on the delta's rows under `cleared`. `all` is the four side by side on one axis, the union of the delta dates, observation days or contract periods, and the payment dates at the level chosen, a month or a year row meaning the pricing contract's period for the deltas and the payment's period for the DV01, each vector copied through places the encoding saved and zero where only the other kind has a date, the cross-gamma's column last and placed as the delta's, a cleared batch's all on the delta axis with a DV01 column and a cross-gamma column of nothing. The Total row is the scalars the evaluation stored, `lot_delta_total`, `discounted_lot_delta_total`, `dv01` and `cross_gamma_total`, summed by no one here; the day rows sum to it, and so do the month rows and the year rows, each column on its own. Since #186 the month rows no longer sum the day rows month by month: a November contract's lots stand in November whatever October days it is observed on, so the day view says when exposure fixes and the month and year views which contracts carry it, and the three agree on the Total alone. A report values nothing, converts nothing, sorts nothing and bumps nothing. The delta by contract and the sensitivity by contract are the readback's, `util_ReadHandle` on the results, with `lot_unit` and `sensitivity_unit` beside them.

## What it reads

The encoding's delta axes: since #166 the distinct observation days, increasing, with each slot's day and each day's pricing contract; since #186 the contract months and years, the first day of the calendar month of each distinct pricing contract among the references, the terms' schedule months gathered by reference, increasing, with each reference's place, and the first day of each of those months' calendar years with each month's place, two uniques over the references and none over the slots. Since #178, for a bilateral financial batch, the payment axes beside them, `PaymentAxes` in `trades/encoding.py`: the distinct payment days of every group, paid or not, their months and their years, three uniques, and at each level the union of the delta axis and the payment axis with each axis's places on it, three unions and six searches, made once when the batch is made and counted as `encoded_payment_days`; a cleared batch, whose DV01 has no dated axis, and a physical one make none. The preparation's three nominal vectors, the live lots summed onto the days and the lot delta by reference summed onto the contract months and then the years, shared by every result over it. The result's three discounted vectors, and since #178 its DV01 by payment day, month and year, the groups' DV01s summed onto the payment axes by three more bincounts, `risk_reductions` 7 where it was 4, each view checked finite and frozen; a cleared result's are None. The result's cross-gamma by reference, by observation day and by contract month and year, the slots' cross-gammas summed onto the delta's axes by four more bincounts, `risk_reductions` 11 where it was 7, each view checked finite and frozen, a cleared result's None, the known zero, and `early_cross_gamma`, the same facts for it. Since #186 the preparation's display axes, `PeriodAxis` in `trades/display_axes.py`, one for the month and one for the year: the consecutive periods from the valuation day's through the farthest saved period, the delta's rows through the last contract period and the DV01's through the last payment period, each saved period's place on the axis and the first saved period on or after the start, two searches a bucket, made once when the batch is prepared and counted as `display_periods`; and `EarlyRisk`, the facts of a saved value before the start, the nominal lots' found by the preparation, the discounted lots' and the DV01's by the evaluation, a cleared result's delta sharing the nominal facts, worded by the reader with the measure asked for. ## Not here

A dense daily calendar: the day view stays the dates that hold something. No cross-gamma to a node or by a bump, and none summed across an index's curves: an index on two curves is two columns. A display start the user chooses, or one from the sheet's clock: the valuation day decides. No grand total across unrelated lots: a collection's report has one column per index, [aggregate-results.md](aggregate-results.md), since #180, and a single result's one; no third kind of results; no quarter or week; no curve identity or currency system, `curve_1` a label; no key-rate DV01, no bump, no scenario; no attribution to a trade or a method; no roll-off forecast; no display name on the labels, the index's name as made.
